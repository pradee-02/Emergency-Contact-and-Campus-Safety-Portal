Campus Safety Guidelines & Protocols

Practical instructions, incident prevention best practices, and emergency action plans.

Emergency Directory
In an Immediate Life-Threatening Situation: Do not wait for a web form response. Immediately contact the 24/7 Campus Control Room at 0422-2614000 or local emergency dispatch (112).
Medical Emergencies
  • Call the Health Centre: Contact the campus dispensary or medical ward at 0422-2614108 immediately.
  • Do Not Move Victims: If spinal trauma, heavy bleeding, or fracture is suspected, keep the individual stationary unless environmental threat persists.
  • First Aid Kits: Accessible in all laboratory corridors, department offices, and sports complexes.
Fire Hazards & Evacuation
  • Pull the Alarm: Activate the nearest manual break-glass call point.
  • Stairs Only: Never utilize elevators during an active fire or electrical outage.
  • Designated Assembly Points: Assemble at open sports grounds or marked campus quadrangles. Remain accounted for.
Harassment & Anti-Ragging
  • Strict Zero-Tolerance: University policy strictly criminalizes physical or psychological bullying, intimidation, and unauthorized hazing.
  • Confidential Support: Contact the Women's Safety Cell or counseling mentors; identity protection is safeguarded.
  • Document Evidence: Preserve electronic messages, screenshots, dates, and names of individuals present.
Suspicious Activities
  • Unattended Items: Never touch unattended packages, luggage, or suspicious parcels in academic buildings.
  • Unauthorized Personnel: Report individuals loitering near residential dormitories or restricted lab rooms without valid student/staff IDs.
  • Log Details: Note clothing, vehicle license plates, or specific directions of movement.
Theft Prevention
  • Secure Belongings: Never leave laptops, mobile phones, or backpacks unattended in libraries, cafeteria booths, or lecture halls.
  • Bicycle Locks: Always lock bicycles to designated metal racks using a hardened U-lock or reinforced steel cable.
  • Report Immediately: Log theft reports on this portal to trigger security camera audit requests.
Nighttime Movement
  • Well-Lit Pathways: Stick to arterial campus roads lined with active security lighting. Avoid unlit shortcuts or dense tree corridors after sunset.
  • Buddy System: Walk with peers when returning from evening library study hours or lab sessions.
  • Security Patrol Escort: Campus security patrol vehicles are available to escort students between blocks during late hours.

Responsible Incident Reporting

When filing an incident report via CampusSafe, provide factual, objective details without speculative claims. Submitting false reports compromises emergency personnel and wastes critical safety resources.

Data Protection: All incident data is stored securely in your MongoDB Atlas cluster. Passwords are encrypted with cryptographic salts, and student identities are protected in accordance with university confidentiality standards.
