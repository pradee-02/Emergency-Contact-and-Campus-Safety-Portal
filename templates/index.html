REPORTING PROCESS

How to Report a Campus Incident

Simple 3-step structured workflow ensuring your submission is documented, assigned, and resolved.

1
Submit Form

Fill in the incident category, exact campus location, date/time, and urgency. Add optional supporting details.

2
Get Reference ID

The server immediately stores the record in MongoDB Atlas and generates a trackable reference ID (e.g. INC-202610-89F2).

3
Admin Review & Resolution

Campus security officers review the case, update the status to Under Review or Resolved, and log investigation notes.

Medical Emergencies
Campus dispensary & ambulance response

In acute medical situations, sudden fainting, or laboratory accidents, immediately notify the on-campus dispensary and campus security gate to ensure unblocked ambulance access to the building.

  • Do not move injured persons unless immediate hazard exists.
  • Clear the pathway for first responder stretchers.
  • Report exact floor, room number, and landmark.

Harassment & Anti-Ragging Cell
Strict zero-tolerance policy

The university maintains zero tolerance against any form of ragging, verbal intimidation, or gender-based harassment. Confidential support and counseling cells are active across campuses.

  • Safe, confidential lodging of student complaints.
  • Strict disciplinary committee review with anti-ragging squad.
  • Direct counseling and legal support available upon request.
